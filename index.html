  • Popol and Kupa revamp (Popol can now ride Kupa).

Macro play

Map & Rotation Analysis

Patch notes and strategic interpretation from the source file. This is a planning board, not a live minimap or real-time objective timer.

🟢 EXP lane

Check lane priority before rotating. Avoid abandoning a wave just before a major objective unless the trade is deliberate.

🔵 Mid lane

Clear mid before moving. Mid priority enables river vision and faster support to either side lane.

🟡 Gold lane

Protect siege minion income. Source notes say Gold-lane siege minion extra gold rose from 40% to 60%.

🟣 Jungle / river

Track enemy jungler, protect river access and use Revealing Wisps to expose nearby unseen enemies.
